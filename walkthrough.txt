=====================================================================
          WORLD OF STRONKIE REMAKE - CHAPTER ONE: THE PROLOGUE
                        Complete walkthrough
                Created By Mark Stronks, Stronkie Soft
=====================================================================

You can never die and never get stuck. The three star fragments (*)
are hidden in the same places as in the original text adventure.
"Use X with Y": click Use, then X in your inventory, then Y.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PROLOGUE, PART 1: THE GROUNDS   (also the free demo)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Walk through the iron gates into the garden.
- Walk to the garden shed (east).
- Pick up the rusty tools.                     * Star fragment 1
- Pick up the shovel.
- Leave the shed. Use the shovel with the mound of earth:
  you dig up an iron handle. Pick up the iron handle.
- Walk to the old well (west). Use the iron handle with the winch:
  the bucket comes up. Pick up the brass key from the bucket.
- Back to the garden, then to the porch of the manor (north).
- Pick up the doormat: a scrap of paper (a hint from Uncle Edmund).
- Open the front door (or use the brass key with it), and walk in.
  The door slams shut behind you...

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PROLOGUE, PART 2: THE MANOR
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Walk through the door to the east: the kitchen.
- Pick up the wax candle, the box of matches and the kitchen knife.
- Use the matches with the candle: a burning candle.
- Walk down the steps to the cellar.
- Pick up the old barrels.                     * Star fragment 2
- Pick up the silver key. Go back up, and back to the hall.
- Use the silver key with the small door, and walk through it:
  Uncle Edmund's study.
- Look at the diary: "...pull the red book."
- Back to the hall, then through the door to the west: the library.
- Pull the red book: a secret passage opens. Walk through it.
- Use the kitchen knife with the ropes: Uncle Edmund is free!

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PROLOGUE, PART 3: THE LAUNCH PAD
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Uncle Edmund gives you the amulet.
- Pick up the worn steps (search them).        * Star fragment 3
- Walk down the stairs: the steel corridor.
- Walk to the locker room (west). Open the locker marked PILOT:
  a spacesuit and a keycard. Use the spacesuit: you put it on.
- Back to the corridor. Use the keycard with the card slot:
  the blast door opens. Walk through it: the hangar.
- Walk to the control room. (Talk to Uncle Edmund if you like.)
- Use the amulet with the round slot: the roof slides open.
- Pull the big red lever: the STARRAVEN's hatch opens.
- Back to the hangar. Walk to the STARRAVEN (up the ramp).
- Push the LAUNCH button. LIFT-OFF!

To be continued in Chapter Two: Into Space.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
THINGS TO TRY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Look at the well, then try to climb down the rope.
- Use the microwave in the kitchen. Look at the jar in the study.
- Try to pick up the amulet before you free Uncle Edmund.
- Push the LAUNCH button without the flight suit.
- Wait a while on the grounds: lightning, and a face at a window.
